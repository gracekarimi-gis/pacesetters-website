Bare brand, bare numbers, bare journey.

I'm Milka Awuor Oromo — a financial accountant based in Germany by day, and the founder of The Pacesetters. What started in my free time became an educational and motivational platform built to inspire and empower Kenyans living here: I sit down with people doing remarkable things and share their stories, experiences, achievements, and lessons with the community.

I believe in the power of stories, knowledge, and connection to help people pursue their goals and create real change. This site is that mission, out in the open — comment on any episode, react with a sticker, tell me who to feature next. And this is only the start: the vision is to grow The Pacesetters beyond digital storytelling into purpose-driven events that educate, connect, and empower the Kenyan community across Germany.
